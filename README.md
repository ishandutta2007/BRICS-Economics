This repository provides a comprehensive and data-driven comparison of the long-term macroeconomic projections between the expanded BRICS nations and the G7 economies. By leveraging historical inflation rates and real GDP growth metrics, we forecast the economic trajectories, nominal GDP, and Purchasing Power Parity (PPP) up to the year 2050.

> $$ \text{Nominal Growth Rate} = (1 + \text{Real Growth}) \times (1 + \text{Inflation}) - 1 $$
>
> $$ \text{2050 GDP} = \text{2026 GDP} \times (1 + \text{Nominal Growth Rate})^{24} $$
>
>  For simplifiation assuming constant population, so that I can use GDP growth data as GDP per capita growth data
